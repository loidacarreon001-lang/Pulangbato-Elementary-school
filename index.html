About The Hilltop

The online publication of Pulangbato Elementary School, Almeria, Biliran.

Stories that inform, inspire, and unite.

The Hilltop is a school publication space for sharing school news, learner achievements, feature stories, and editorial cartoons. It aims to encourage responsible communication, creativity, literacy, and pride in our school community.
